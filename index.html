Instructions

Saved in this browser. Use the public key (starts with sl_pk_); never the secret one (sl_sk_).

Quick access

Saves your Client ID, your Client Key and your Spotify session to a file so you can sign in quickly in another browser or domain (the browser stores this data separately in each one). The file is equivalent to a password: don’t share it or upload it to any repository.

Appearance

Font

On fonts.google.com pick a font, press “Get font” → “Get embed code” and paste here the link (or the whole code) it gives you. It is applied to the whole app, lyrics included, and saved in this browser.

Lyrics

Lyrics renderer

Spicy is the new renderer, derived from the one in Spicy Lyrics and from the AMLL TTML Tool (AGPL-3.0). Classic is the previous one. The change is applied instantly and saved in this browser.

TV and remote

With the remote: arrows to move, OK to select and Back to go back (in the player, left and right on the progress bar skip forward or back 10 s). The media keys (play/pause, next, previous) also work. Many TVs can’t play audio with the Spotify SDK: in that case turn on remote control, open Spotify on your phone or computer, and the TV will show the lyrics and control that playback. Remote control needs Spotify Premium.

Custom CSS

Applied instantly and saved in this browser. The base includes commented examples (font, variables, shapes): remove the comment from whatever you want to enable.

Playlists

Queue

Spotify doesn’t let other apps remove or reorder songs in the queue. You can tap a song to jump to it (the ones before it are skipped).
